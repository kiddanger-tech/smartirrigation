Built From Plywood, Not A Parts Catalog

A wide pyramid base keeps it stable in open fields, with everything electronic tucked inside a sealed compartment out of the weather.

Brain

ESP32 Dev Board

Runs the show — reads every sensor, drives the pump relay, and updates the on-board LCD.

Soil

Capacitive Moisture Sensor

Tracks moisture at root depth without corroding like resistive probes.

Air

DHT11 Temp / Humidity

Local climate context — heat stress looks different from drought stress.

Water

JSN-SR04T Ultrasonic

Watertight level sensing for the reservoir with no contact with the water itself.

Control

Relay Pump + Buzzer

Switches irrigation on demand and sounds a local alert when something needs attention.

Vision

ESP32-CAM

Photographs the crop on a schedule, watching for pest damage and rodent activity.

From Soil To Screen, In Five Hops

Every reading and photo makes the same trip — off the field, through analysis, and onto your dashboard.

01

Sense

ESP32 polls soil, water, temperature and humidity sensors.

02

Capture

ESP32-CAM snaps a crop photo, checking for pests and rodents.

03

Sync

Readings and images upload straight to Supabase.

04

Analyze

Gemini reviews each photo and payload for plant health signals.

05

Act

You watch it live below, and toggle the pump remotely.
